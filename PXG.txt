Paris X Gen (PXG) is one of the top teams in the Blue Lock project, featuring several key players. 
Notable members include Rin Itoshi, Zantetsu Tsurugi, Julian Loki, and Tabito Karasu. 
Other players include Akira Endoji, Cho Kento, Nijiro Nanase, Hyoma Chigiri, Ikki Niko, Jin Kiyora, Jingo Raichi, Seishiro Nagi, and Junichi Wanima
